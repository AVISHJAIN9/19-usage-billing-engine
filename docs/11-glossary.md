# Technical Glossary

- **Metering**: The process of tracking, recording, and attributing real-time resource consumption (API calls, storage, compute seconds) to specific tenants and customers.
- **Rating**: The mathematical translation of raw consumption units into monetary currency according to contract rules, pricing tiers, and discounts.
- **Idempotency Key**: A unique client-generated token accompanying a request, ensuring that retried network submissions do not cause duplicate processing or double-billing.
- **Graduated (Bracket) Pricing**: A tiered pricing structure where usage is sliced across progressive thresholds, with different unit rates applied to each successive volume bracket.
- **Volume Pricing**: A pricing model where the total volume achieved over the billing period determines a single discounted rate applied to all consumed units.
- **Overage Pricing**: A hybrid model offering a fixed base fee covering an included quantity of units, with excess consumption billed at a specified overage rate.
- **Double-Entry Bookkeeping**: An accounting system where every financial transaction consists of at least two entries, guaranteeing that the sum of debits strictly equals the sum of credits.
- **Gapless Sequential Numbering**: A legal requirement in fiscal accounting ensuring that invoice numbers follow an unbroken arithmetic sequence (e.g., `INV-2026-0001`, `INV-2026-0002`) with no missing numbers.
- **Dunning**: The automated process of communicating with customers and scheduling payment retries to collect outstanding balances on past-due invoices.
- **Proration**: The proportional allocation of subscription charges or credits based on the exact elapsed time when a customer changes plans mid-cycle.
- **Row-Level Security (RLS)**: A database security mechanism restricting data access to rows matching the authenticated tenant's identity.
- **Differential Testing**: A software testing technique where the output of an optimized operational system (e.g. pre-aggregated rollups) is verified against an unoptimized reference implementation (e.g. from-scratch SQL sums).
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: The predictable recurring revenue generated by active customer subscriptions normalized to a monthly interval.
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: The annualized value of recurring customer subscriptions ($\text{MRR} \times 12$).
